Daily claims v3 setup

Steps 1 to 4 must be signed by the owner wallet 0xD38ffFABf5087489ae1486c7Aa9B6a993c819f37; step 5 by any wallet that holds USDG.

Not connected.

1. Set the poster

The server key that posts the daily root. It can do nothing else.

2. Set the poster's limits

How much one root may add, and how often the poster may post.

3. Set the payout budget

The most USDG all claims together may take per window. This is what bounds a loss if the poster key or a root is ever wrong. No claims are possible until it is set.

4. Retire v2

Pause v2 0x225a42c80e3dad180067e02461de7149ebad951f, then move its spare USDG back to the owner wallet. v3 subtracts what v2 already paid.

5. Fund v3

USDG for the paybacks. Keep it to a few days of paybacks at a time.

6. Post the owner root

The first root, or one over the poster's cap. The daily job built and stored it; this sends its setRoot from the owner wallet.
